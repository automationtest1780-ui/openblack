# Act IV starting state (end of Act III, evening of day 28)

Written by Claude on 2026-10-07 after the Act III continuity pass, to brief the Interlude III/IV and Act IV writers. It summarizes canon. The ledger, the sheets and patches 301-306 win over this page; the world bible wins over all of them.

## The sky and the sea
- **Belt:** dusk bearing **12** (old notch 41). Shifts: before night three (41 → 37), dusk day 13 (36 → 32), about 2 a.m. night of day 17 (32 → 28), dusk day 20 (28 → 24), dusk day 22 (24 → 20), **about midnight night of day 24 (20 → 16)**, **dusk day 26 at the crater (16 → 12)**. Gaps: 16 days, 4, 2¾, 2, 2¼ ("the long one", unexplained), 1¾.
- **The still night:** the 8th shift was due about 2 a.m. on the night of day 27 (Sera two, Ivo a quarter to two). It **did not come**. The Belt held at 12 from dusk on day 27 to dawn on day 28, and was still 12 at dusk on day 28 (306). Ivo: "It was late. Nobody said it was cancelled." Wren: "It's only waiting." The next shift is overdue, and nobody on screen has said when.
- **Between shifts** the Belt creeps a few minutes a night, and almost nothing on nights both Sera and Ivo counted (Sera's column, 304). Observation only; nobody says why (OQ 4).
- **Tide:** the water runs more than four hours ahead of Sera's old (struck-through) tables, about fifteen minutes more across each shift and about five on a quiet night. The spring tide flooded the customs house at a quarter past two on day 25 (table: twenty to seven). On the night of day 27 high water came after three, with Jory reading the gauge every half hour. Nobody prints tables; Sera reads the breakwater log every morning and copies it onto paper.
- **The bloom:** the dark two fingers past the Belt's end star and up a hair is "opening" (Wren). Sera can measure nothing there. The thrift by the tower door, the flower behind the word, is fully open.

## The count, the chalk, the ledger, the fair copy
- **Count:** on paper. Sera's tide book holds two columns, "S.V." and "I.T.", with Ivo's figures in her hand beside hers; she also writes in the fair copy's margin. Bram's slip readings go in it too ("Bram. Three. Fourth step."). She is copying Ivo's first two weeks into the front of it.
- **Wall:** full since day 22. **Glass:** "bloom" in chalk by the second window bar, and the 41 strokes of the still night under it ("Don't wipe the glass.").
- **Chalk:** **the keeper holds it** again ("Hold the chalk."; "Keep it. Dusk. And after.").
- **Ledger:** council property. It lives on the customs house table, open to any resident. Ivo signed it out "to myself, as chair" at dusk on day 28 and takes it back each morning. Sera has asked for it at dusk on day 29 ("You'll be on time for then."). It is not handed over until Act V. Last page blank.
- **Fair copy:** the clerk's sky figures (no tides, no margins), string-tied once, now opened; on the lamp-room cradle step.

## Wren
- **Brightening, edging to Called.** An older teen; she looked Sera level in the eye after the 6th shift and has grown since; the coat sleeves end near the elbow. She gives a steady light of her own in the dark, enough to see her face by, and **sees in the dark** (reeds one at a time, a watch face, Jory's pencil). She hears slow feet far down the stair. She warmed a pot of broth by holding it ("It's not a shift. I'm only warm."). Not yet bright enough to read by (that's 4-1).
- Itches before a shift, from noon; on the still night she felt it "lift its foot" and "put it down. Same place."
- **Memories** of up there, sensory only: a wide cold that ticked; full quiet; "Everything lit, and nothing a lamp"; a crowd of lights standing still with nobody counting them; "no down... they just held their places"; "all outside". Down here starts with Bram's coat. She doesn't know what she is.
- **First to say "the bloom"** (to Corra, in the crater bowl). She keeps Bram's words in order (sky, gull, smoke; supper, thrift, bloom). Counted the stair 108 for the first time (305). Called every minute of the due hour on Ivo's watch, and gave it back at dusk on day 28.
- Asked Ivo "Are you frightened of me?" ("...Yes."). She is "under 'almost.' Near the top." In the ledger she was "it" on night three.

## The Lamp Room four and the keeper
- **Sera:** right, and it weighs nothing ("Like twelve."); the town staying up weighed more. Speaks to Ivo directly and by name since 303/305; uses the post-Act III bark ("Call it."). Has not forgiven him ("No." / "Good."). The deal with Bram is half-restored (she said the first half, 305); the leaf is "for after supper". Eats her supper hot now; the tally keeps its stroke.
- **Bram:** on "supper's side", "on the stair": he refused to pick between Sera and Ivo, to both. Held the light all night where Sera needs it. Missed his three o'clock at the slip once. Gave Wren the word "bloom" and measured her with his thumb (near the elbow), telling only the keeper. Nell's berth: "Ask me on the thirtieth. I'll be at the slip. To wave. Whichever." Twitched at Ivo's "In case." (oil cupboard, untouched).
- **Ivo:** confessed in open council in the flood (302); said "I am sorry" once (303); told the town to look up, in red (305); counted the still night with Sera and wrote "Held." but not why. Mags counted him once. Hester keeps him under "took", new column pending. No contractions; "I do not know" now only when true. Still privately sends money to the Flints (never on screen in Part 1).
- **The keeper:** holds the chalk; drew the 41 strokes; gave the second sight ("Three fingers." / "Still three."). Knows everything Bram knew about the ledger, and carried the fair copy up unsigned. Still not praised.

## The launch heroes
- **Nell:** **"The thirtieth, first light."** *Patience* and five more boats (the Harbormaster's two among them) are loaded. At dawn on day 28: "Where's south, now?" The date stands. Roadmap 4-3 has her turn back "not tonight"; Act IV must pass or meet the thirtieth on screen.
- **Tam:** hands still bandaged and itching, "weeks" yet; won't go south; stays and counts (the loft door is chalked).
- **Hester:** fed the harbor on one slate on the harbor wall; has the "Don't look up" sheet in her apron; LAMP ROOM on the pot lid "till Saltreach works out who's speaking to who".
- **Jory:** his father was cleared in public (302). Silent for a day, now speaking again ("Didn't feel like anything. Thought it would."). Keeps the breakwater lamp and the gauge, signs "J. Flint", answered the tower's dips. His second log line of day 25 is unread. He still doesn't know who sends the envelopes.
- **Mags:** counts at two every night; counted Wren by name, and Ivo once. "Thinking" about Teodor's offer.
- **Corra:** the only witness to "the bloom", and believed it. Held Wren's hand; climbed the stair, 108; Sera lets her up ("Then come."). Twelve and "all night" on the pan-house door, room for two more. Her night-of-the-fall secret is kept.
- **Abel:** dug and failed to fill the crater ("A hole's a thing I can fill."); looked up at the window, then at the sky on purpose. His stakes keep being walked through; the reeds lean toward the town.
- **Teodor:** offered to buy every piece of glass and "the bowl as well"; refused under the glass stop; "Where I'm from, nobody stayed up to find out." On day 28 he'd "rather not have it". Cases unseen; Bellwater locked.

## The council and the town
- **Council:** three seats and the chair. The Harbormaster (sailing on the thirtieth), the Chandler and the Shipwright walked out; Gage stays and minutes in his own style ("Item. Nothing happened. All night. Whole harbor present."). The glass stop stands; the oil ration stands except for the one lifted night. **LOOK UP** in red is the only notice on the door.
- **Mood:** three camps (leave with the fleet, fill the crater, stay and count), but the whole harbor stayed up on the wall one night and saw the sky hold. Doors are chalked with the Belt's bearings. People look up after supper now. The crater is not filled: a hand of sand, a cart in the bowl, a walker's 32 prints.
- **Enemies:** Haze-Walkers (walk straight lines; round lamplight in the open, stop at its edge on a stair; stood facing the tower at the 7th shift and facing Wren's gold on the pans). Tide-Plumbs came in with the spring flood. None speak.

2. Sera and Ivo counting together nightly (the ledger at dusk on day 29); forgiveness not given; Ivo's "Ivo, the Truth-Teller" interlude (answering every question, 2.1).
3. Wren's itch, her "waiting", her memories (OQ 44); 4-1 lets her say plainly she came from the bloom and it wants her back.
4. Sera and Bram: the leaf is unsettled; the deal's second half; Bram letting go (4-4).
5. Nell and the thirtieth; Bram's answer at the slip; Tam's hands; Corra's family on the slate; Teodor's offer and the glass stop.
6. Jory's second log line (OQ 47); his envelopes stay off screen.
7. Haze-Walker rules, the tall pacer, the 32 steps, the fifteen-minute tide jumps (OQ 39-43, 48).
8. Hester's new column for Ivo.
