## Chapters and handoffs

**2-1 Early Water** (days 16–17, into the small hours of 18)
- Starts: day 16. The notice says "Weather. The council is watching it." Belt 32. Wall: one clean course, its first mark "19". Wren three rolls. Crater Patrols exist (Bram and the keeper walk the crater road at dusk).
- Owns: the tide 2 h 10 early on the night of day 17; *Patience* nearly founders on the bar (Nell saved by the breakwater lamp and the keeper); the 3rd shift ~2 a.m. seen through cloud.
- **The hidden reading:** at 3 a.m. the keeper logs the breakwater gauge at 2 h 40 ahead and still rising, with the line "Tables not usable." Bram, alone with the keeper, cuts that leaf out of the breakwater log with his knife and puts it in his oilskin. He asks the keeper to say nothing ("She'll go and stand in it"). The keeper's choice is non-branching (silence either way). Sera never sees the 2 h 40 figure.
- Jory reads the log the next evening (his father taught him) and notices a cut leaf by the stitching. He says nothing yet.
- Ends: morning of day 18, Wren not yet seen awake (2-2 opens on her waking).

**2-2 Kindling** (days 18–20)
- Starts: morning of day 18. Wren wakes at teenage height, sleeves one roll; she's thrilled, Bram goes quiet.
- Owns: Wren runs to show Tam; the town begins to notice her; the 4th shift at dusk on day 20 hits while she is holding Tam's hands on the net-loft stair, she flares, and his hands burn; Hester dresses them in the smokehouse while half the harbor watches; **Bram's first "Sparrow"**; "Don't look up after supper" starts (a saying among the harbor women, not yet the council's). After this shift her coat fits, no rolls.
- Wren grows *during* a shift in front of people: that's how the town learns "she grows when the sky moves" (Act II reveal; people may say it, nobody explains it).
- Ends: night of day 20. Tam can't mend for weeks.

**2-3 Don't Look Up After Supper** (day 21)
- Starts: morning of day 21, the harbor afraid of the sky and of Wren.
- Owns: Ivo's measures in council: a lamp-oil ration that keeps the town indoors after dark (the harbor fund has eleven weeks of oil), and a stop on crater-glass sales "for public order". It works; the marsh turns on him (Mags must tell her cutters there's no glass money; Corra's family hit; Teodor unbothered, waiting). Ivo adopts the harbor women's saying as policy: "Don't look up after supper." First **Tide-Plumbs** stand in the harbor at the evening tide. That night in the lamp room Sera chalks Wren's height on the wall, on clean stone, as data; Wren stands very straight; Bram calls Sera on it ("She's not a reading"). Sera doesn't answer.
- Ends: night of day 21. The height mark has used part of the last course.

**2-4 Out of Wall** (day 22, Act II finale)
- Starts: day 22.
- Owns: at dusk the Plumbs stand in straight lines across the marsh, all facing the lighthouse; **Abel is the first to say out loud they're measuring something** (Act II reveal; he doesn't say what); the 5th shift; Sera chalks the last clean stone; Jory brings the breakwater log up the stair and shows the cut leaf; Bram takes the leaf out of his oilskin; "2 h 40. Tables not usable."; Sera and Bram's worst quarrel, staged with no music. Bram still brings supper that night; Sera doesn't touch it (her tally still gets its stroke).
- Ends: night of day 22.

**Quartet scene 2, "The Last Clean Stone"** (evening of day 23)
- The wall is full. They argue about where to write next. Wren offers her arm. Sera and Bram are still raw from the quarrel. No plot that exists nowhere else.

## Rules that hold all act
- Reveal gate Act II: the sky is speeding up; Wren grows with every shift; the Plumbs seem to be measuring (said by Abel first, in 2-4); the wall runs out. **No ledger, no "Bloom", no explanation of why.** Plumbs, Tide-Plumbs and Glass Hounds never speak.
- Sera still never speaks directly to Ivo (pre-Act III). Ivo never says "haze" in private and never shows a sky ledger.
- Wren is **Kindling**: teenage, still quick and cheerful; now and then a word that "comes from too far up". Her scariest lines still end on a question or a joke.
- Bond secrets stay secret: Bram's oil cupboard, Sera's supper tally, Wren's marks under the stair, Ivo's money, Jory's envelopes, Teodor's cases.
- Bram never lies outright to Sera's face about the reading before 2-4. He avoids, jokes and changes the subject.
