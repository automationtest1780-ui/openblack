## 1. Overview

### 1.1 What Part 1 is
- **Part 1 = Saltreach:** the Opening plus Acts I-V (project.md hierarchy). Each act is an arc of 3-6 chapters; each chapter is one release drop (gacha 3.1).
- **Count:** Opening + 21 chapters (I: 4, II: 4, III: 5, IV: 4, V: 4) + 2 interludes (III/IV, and "Part 1.5" after the ending).
- **Spine:** the Lamp Room four carry every chapter (gacha 3.5: free leads stay central). Launch pullables join chapters as featured heroes; none is ever required (Hard rule 1).
- **Meta-mystery:** one only (gacha 3.2): *what is moving the sky, and what is Wren?* Each act closes locally and moves it one step (WB §3 table).
- **Campaign clock:** each act covers about half the in-world time of the one before (WB §7): Act I weeks, Act II a couple of weeks, Act III days, Act IV a night or two, Act V one night. Real-world release pacing is separate.

### 1.2 Version mapping (design assumptions: ~6-week versions, two ~3-week phases)
- **Phase 1** of each version opens the main-story beat (chapter or interlude). **Phase 2** carries the flagship event (gacha 3.4, 9.1).
- **Finale versions** (an act's last chapter) drop that version's event to festival or light weight (gacha 3.4 finale exception).
- **Cadence:** one main beat per version after launch. Story-first pacing is acceptable (gacha 3.4); events and group stories fill gaps.
- **Anchor points:** Act II finale lands on the half-anniversary (1.4); Act III finale ("the sky holds still") lands on the first anniversary (2.0); Act V finale lands on the second anniversary (2.8), which doubles as the Part 1 finale (gacha 3.6).

### 1.3 What ships at launch (version 1.0)
- **Opening** (patch 000, needs its VN-budget revision pass first) to the first summon at scene 7/8, under ~30 minutes.
- **All of Act I** (4 chapters, ~2-3.5 hours VN), so a new player gets a complete arc with a real close (the visible shift, Wren taller) before the first wait.
- **Quartet scene 1** "Supper in the Lamp Room" (group story, unlocks at Act I clear).
- **12 heroes:** the Lamp Room four (free) + Net Lofts four + Salt Pans four (pullable). Baseline package (gacha 7.1) for all eight pullables at launch.
- **Beginner banner:** guaranteed pick of one of the eight pullables, framed "someone else in Saltreach who saw the light fall" (OQ 7).
- **Launch festival** (phase 2): The Smokehouse Feast (see 4.2).
- **Recommended rarity** (design sets it): top rarity Nell, Jory, Corra, Teodor; the other four one tier down. Roster.md suggests two top-rarity per pullable quartet; this picks them.
