# Groups (Saltreach launch quartets)

Saltreach launches with twelve heroes in three quartets, each living with the moving sky its own way (roster.md; WB §8 "Building New Quartets"). The **Lamp Room** *counts* the sky. They're the free starter quartet, split by truth vs protection, with Bram as heart and Wren as question. The **Net Lofts** *steer* by it, and now it's wrong: the fleet argues leave vs stay, with Hester holding the middle and Jory, the keeper's returned son, as the question. The **Salt Pans** had it *land* on their doorstep: the marsh argues sell the glass vs bury it, with Mags holding the middle and Teodor, the Bellwater buyer, as the question. Each file gives the place, fault line, heart and question, the six-pair matrix with barks (versioned after arc beats where the relationship changes), and the cross-quartet pairs to reach for in events.
