Status: confirmed (canon + working from world bible). Pairings and barks are copied from world-bible.md §5; quartet scenes from §6. Cross-quartet pairs are proposed (Claude, on user's delegation, 2026-10-06).
Release: starter quartet, free through the story (open question 7). Gacha 8.

## Identity
- **Place:** the top of the dead lighthouse on the headland: a round room of salt-stained glass, an empty lamp cradle, and Sera's chalk wall. Home base. (WB §2)
- **How they feel the sky:** they *count* it. Sera chalks the drift every dusk; Ivo has a ledger he hasn't shown anyone; Bram keeps the light for when they come down; and Wren fell out of it.
- **Fault line:** truth vs protection. Sera and Ivo, and the lie. (WB §5)
- **Heart:** Bram. He holds the middle.
- **Question:** Wren. The thing the others must eventually decide about together.

## Pair matrix (copied from WB §5)

### Sera and Bram
- **Dynamic:** Supper every night for as long as the wall has been filling. She trusts him completely. He loves her and has never said so.
- **Tension:** He wants her to look down. She wants him to look up.
- **Bark:**
  - Bram: "Eyes up there, feet down here, deal?"
  - Sera: "Deal. Stay in the light."

### Sera and Ivo (the fault line)
- **Dynamic:** Two people holding the same numbers. She told the truth and was mocked. He lied and was believed. He respects her more than anyone alive. She refuses to forgive him.
- **Tension:** The lie. Before Act III, she resents him for dismissing her. After Act III, she knows he *knew*, which is worse.
- **Bark (pre-Act III):**
  - Ivo: "Your mark is two degrees off."
  - Sera: "Then it's two degrees closer than yours."
- **Bark (post-Act III):**
  - Sera: "Call it."
  - Ivo: "...Thank you. Left."

### Sera and Wren
- **Dynamic:** Wren worships Sera. To Sera, Wren is proof, a walking measurement that she was right.
- **Tension:** Sera keeps measuring Wren's height on the lamp-room wall, and Wren starts to realize she's being recorded rather than loved.
- **Bark:**
  - Wren: "Did you see? Did you count it?"
  - Sera: "I counted. That was well done." *(The second sentence is the growth.)*

### Bram and Ivo
- **Dynamic:** They've known each other for years. Ivo signed the order that closed the old light, and Bram forgave him for it long ago, which Ivo has never understood.
- **Tension:** Ivo might confess to Bram first (he does, mid Act III: open question 2). When the lie comes out, Bram refuses to pick a side.
- **Bark:**
  - Bram: "You're doing that face where you've already won."
  - Ivo: "It's my only face, Hollis."

### Bram and Wren
- **Dynamic:** He found her, named her, and gave her his coat. She calls him family, the first word she learned for it.
- **Tension:** Each night the coat fits her a little better, and the girl he found slips further away.
- **Bark:**
  - Wren: "Bram! I'm bigger than the lantern now!"
  - Bram: "Then you'd better stay where I can see you, Sparrow."

### Ivo and Wren
- **Dynamic:** He thinks she's the most dangerous thing in the harbor. She is desperate to make him smile.
- **Tension:** He's right that she's dangerous, and wrong about what to do about her. He argues she should be sent up (Act IV). Her attempts to make him smile are a running comic thread that turns sad later.
- **Bark:**
  - Wren: "Was that funny? Did you almost smile?"
  - Ivo: "I noted it. Under 'almost.'"
