# The Salt Pans

Status: proposed (Claude, on user's delegation, 2026-10-06). Built from roster.md (Quartet 3) by WB §8 "Building New Quartets".
Release: pullable quartet at launch (gacha 8.1). Suggested featured order: Corra (1.0 phase 2, with Abel and Mags), then Teodor (1.1 phase 2), after the others mention him.

## Identity
- **Place:** the salt pans, reed beds and tidal channels north of town, closest to the crater. The pan-house, where Mags keeps the book.
- **How they feel the sky:** it *landed* on their doorstep. The reeds lean, the Plumbs walk the channels, and the crater glass is worth money.
- **Fault line:** sell the glass vs bury it. Crater glass is the first real money the marsh has seen in years, and it's also clearly wrong. **Corra** (sell) vs **Abel** (bury).
- **Heart:** Mags. Takes a share of the glass money for the pan-house, and takes Abel's warnings seriously.
- **Question:** Teodor. Do they let him, and the glass, leave Saltreach? What he knows stays locked until Bellwater's arc (Part 2).
- **Arc beats the barks track:** Act II (Wren hurts someone by accident while glowing; the town notices her), Act IV (Wren is the sky's child, and the crater is where she came from).

## Pair matrix

### Corra and Abel (the fault line)
- **Dynamic:** He taught her to cut reeds when she was six. Now she sells the thing he wants buried.
- **Tension:** She thinks he'd keep her poor to keep the marsh the way he likes it. He thinks she's selling something that will come back for her.
- **Bark:**
  - Abel: "Put it back, child. Marsh gives, marsh keeps."
  - Corra: "Marsh never gave me a thing. This did."
- **Bark (after Act II, once Wren has hurt someone while glowing):**
  - Corra: "...You think the glass does that? To people?"
  - Abel: "I think it came out of the same hole."

### Corra and Mags
- **Dynamic:** Mags is her boss, and covers for her with her mother.
- **Tension:** Mags takes a pan-house share of the glass money. Fair's fair, says Mags. Corra thinks it was her pan and her apron.
- **Bark:**
  - Mags: "Pan-house share, Corra. Fair's fair."
  - Corra: "Fair's whatever the one holding the tin says."

### Corra and Teodor
- **Dynamic:** He's her buyer. He pays well and calls her "Miss Vey", which nobody has ever called her. She likes being treated as a dealer.
- **Tension:** She suspects he pays too well, and she's afraid to ask why, in case he stops.
- **Bark:**
  - Teodor: "A lovely piece, Miss Vey. I'll pay what it's worth."
  - Corra: "Pay what you paid yesterday. And a bit."
- **Bark (after Act IV, once everyone knows where Wren came from):**
  - Corra: "What's it worth now, Mr Lisle?"
  - Teodor: "Less than you'd think. More than I'd like."

### Abel and Mags
- **Dynamic:** Thirty years running the pans together. She's the boss and he's older, and neither of them mentions it.
- **Tension:** She believes him about the glass. She still can't bury it, because the marsh needs the money this winter.
- **Bark:**
  - Abel: "Reeds leaned again."
  - Mags: "I know, Abel. Cut them anyway."

### Abel and Teodor
- **Dynamic:** Abel distrusts anyone who buys what ought to be buried. Teodor is unfailingly polite to him, and Abel's pony likes Teodor, which Abel takes as a betrayal.
- **Tension:** Teodor is the only person who doesn't argue with Abel. He quietly agrees the glass is wrong, and keeps buying it.
- **Bark:**
  - Abel: "My pony likes you. I don't hold with it."
  - Teodor: "Animals are generous, Mr Thorn. I try to deserve it."

### Mags and Teodor
- **Dynamic:** Business partners. She sets the marsh's price and he never haggles. She likes him, and doesn't trust that she likes him.
- **Tension:** She's the one who'll decide whether he, and the glass, leave.
- **Bark:**
  - Mags: "Price went up. Weather's turned."
  - Teodor: "Weather's been turning a long while, Mrs Teller."
