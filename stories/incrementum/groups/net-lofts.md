# The Net Lofts

Status: proposed (Claude, on user's delegation, 2026-10-06). Built from roster.md (Quartet 2) by WB §8 "Building New Quartets".
Release: pullable quartet at launch (gacha 8.1). Suggested featured order: Nell (1.0 phase 1, with Tam and Hester), then Jory (1.1 phase 1), so the others mention him first.

## Identity
- **Place:** the harbor: the net lofts above the slips, Hester's smokehouse, the boats. *Patience* is the last big herring boat.
- **How they feel the sky:** they *steer* by it. The bad year already broke the fleet once, and now the stars they navigate by are wrong. Pell's six pots (000) are the first loss; theirs come next.
- **Fault line:** leave vs stay. Does the fleet go south where the herring went, or does Saltreach hold? **Nell** (leave) vs **Tam** (stay).
- **Heart:** Hester. Feeds both sides on credit and asks neither to choose.
- **Question:** Jory. The town can't decide whether to take him back, and the fleet's question (do you abandon a place?) is the one his family already answered.
- **Arc beats the barks track:** Act II (tides fall off the tables; boats nearly lost), Act III (the ledger ties the bad year to the sky: the herring didn't simply go south), and the Net Lofts group story (proposed: whether *Patience* sails, and whether Jory stays; outcome not decided).

## Pair matrix

### Nell and Tam (the fault line)
- **Dynamic:** She's known him since he was a boy tangled in her nets. He mends hers best, and she wants him on *Patience* when she goes south. He won't go past the breakwater.
- **Tension:** She thinks staying is sentiment. He thinks leaving is giving up on something only torn.
- **Bark:**
  - Nell: "Rook. South's warm. Nets tear there too."
  - Tam: "Then they won't need me, will they."
- **Bark (after Act II, when the boats are nearly lost on a flat sea):**
  - Tam: "You came back in. That's twice now."
  - Nell: "Don't keep score. Haul."

### Nell and Hester
- **Dynamic:** Same age, old friends. Hester has fed Nell's crew on credit all year; *Patience*'s tab is the biggest on the slate.
- **Tension:** If Nell takes the fleet south, the smokehouse dies. Hester won't ask her to stay, because Hester never asks.
- **Bark:**
  - Hester: "Your crew ate a week on my slate, Garrow."
  - Nell: "Put it on next season. Done."
- **Bark (after Act III, once the bad year is tied to the sky):**
  - Hester: "Still going south?"
  - Nell: "Where's south, now?"
  - *Live from Act III clear: first played at dawn on day 28 (305). 3-2 earned it (the reading) and held it for the hub.*

### Nell and Jory
- **Dynamic:** Nell crewed out of the breakwater when his father kept it, and said hard things about the Flints when they left. Now she's planning the same thing in daylight.
- **Tension:** He's the living proof of what leaving does to the ones who come back. She can't stand being told so by a boy.
- **Bark:**
  - Jory: "Leaving at night, Skipper? That's how my family does it."
  - Nell: "I'll leave at noon and wave."

### Tam and Hester
- **Dynamic:** The net loft is over the smokehouse. She half raised him; he keeps her aprons mended. He tells her everything.
- **Tension:** She thinks his "everything mends" will break him the first time something doesn't.
- **Bark:**
  - Hester: "You've mended that twice, you daft sprat."
  - Tam: "Third time's when it holds."

### Tam and Jory
- **Dynamic:** Boys together on the same street. Tam is the only one in town who greeted Jory as if he'd never left.
- **Tension:** Jory can't stand being forgiven that easily. Tam wants to mend it; Jory wants it left torn so he can stay angry.
- **Bark:**
  - Tam: "Your sleeve's going. I'll do it tonight."
  - Jory: "You don't have to keep doing that."
- **Bark (after the Net Lofts group story, if Jory stays):**
  - Jory: "Sleeve's going, Tam."
  - Tam: "Sit, then."

### Hester and Jory
- **Dynamic:** The Flint family debt is the biggest line on Hester's slate. She has never mentioned it. She fed him the night he got back.
- **Tension:** He knows the line is there and wants to pay it. She won't take it from him, because it was never his debt.
- **Bark:**
  - Jory: "What do we owe you, Hester?"
  - Hester: "You? Nothing. Head down, sprat."
