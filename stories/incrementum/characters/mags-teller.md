Status: proposed (Claude, on user's delegation, 2026-10-06)

- Role: widow who runs the salt pans: wages, shares, quarrels; battle role is shield (shares the damage)
- Group: the Salt Pans (the marsh). The heart: holds the middle between sell and bury
- Rarity and release: standard rarity (suggested). Launch standard pool, featured alongside Corra (1.0, phase 2). In the first-summon pool.
- Banner fantasy: fairness. The boss who holds it together and takes the last share.
- Teaser (proposed): *"Fair's fair. Even when it's rubbish."*
- Want: to keep the marsh fed and fair through the winter
- Need: to let someone else carry a share of her
- Wound: In the bad year she had to tell four families on the pans there was no work, and she did it one kitchen at a time.
- Contradiction: insists everyone takes their fair share, and hasn't taken her own in a year
- Voice — would say: "Fair's fair." / "Half-share." / "Names." / "My lot." / "My feet have opinions about that."
- Voice — would never say: "That's not my problem" / "I deserve it" / a degree, a bearing or odds (she thinks in shares and halves, not measurements) / "I'm tired" straight out (she blames her feet) / anyone's name left off a list
- Verbal tic: counts people by name, out loud, like a roll call ("Corra, Abel, the Dunn boys, me"), and makes tired jokes about her body
- Silence: her own wage. Ask, and she talks about everyone else's.
- Hook behavior: pulls the pencil from behind her ear and taps it on the pay-tin before she speaks
- Bond secret: She hasn't drawn her own wage since the bad year. The pan book shows it paid every week; at night she puts it back in the tin. She's been eating on Hester's slate. *(Why: the heart gives herself the smallest share, quietly. It ties the two quartets through Hester without either one saying so.)*
- Pairs: Corra (boss and the kid she covers for), Abel (thirty years running the pans), Teodor (business partner she likes and doesn't trust liking). Cross-quartet: Hester (the slate). See `groups/salt-pans.md`.
- Alt policy: same person in a new role only (WB §8). Possible return: Mags, on the council, if a salt-pan seat ever opens (working idea).
