# Corra Vey, the Glass-Picker

Status: proposed (Claude, on user's delegation, 2026-10-06)
Pullable: yes (Salt Pans quartet)
Source: roster.md (Quartet 3); world-bible.md §2, §8. Where this sheet and the world bible differ, the world bible wins.

- Role: salt-pan girl, about seventeen, who picks crater glass at dawn and sells it; battle role is burst
- Group: the Salt Pans (the marsh). One side of the fault line: **sell the glass**
- Rarity and release: top rarity (suggested). Headliner of the Salt Pans; featured banner at launch (1.0, phase 2). In the first-summon pool.
- Banner fantasy: the scrappy kid striking it rich. Quick hands, quick mouth, nobody's charity.
- Teaser (proposed): *"It fell on my pan. Finders keepers."*
- Want: to keep selling the glass until her family never has to count pennies again
- Need: to stop treating what she did that night as a price she's already paid
- Wound: The fall knocked her flat on her own pan, and the first thing she did when she got up was fill her apron.
- Contradiction: defiant about the money in daylight, and guilty about it every night
- Voice — would say: "That's a week of bread." / "Finders keepers." / "Keep" (her short name for the Keeper) / "Sera says..." / "What's it worth?"
- Voice — would never say: "I'm sorry" (she says "Fine. Fine!") / "I don't need it" / "Bloom" (before Act III; she calls it "up there") / anything slow / a word she'd have to look up
- Verbal tic: prices everything ("That's two boots and a loaf"), fast and sure, then a beat too late a small honest thing slips out
- Silence: what the glass feels like in her hand. She'll tell you its price, never that.
- Hook behavior: rattles the glass in her apron pocket before she speaks
- Bond secret: That night, with her apron full, she heard a small voice out in the reeds by the crater, asking questions. She went the other way. She found out later whose voice it was, and has never told anyone, least of all Wren. *(Why: it's the guilt "underneath" from the roster, it's about a person not the sky, and it gives Corra and Wren a scene that matters. See Locked until for how it fits the canon that Bram reached the crater first.)*
- Pairs: Abel (the fault line: sell vs bury), Mags (her boss, who takes a share and covers for her), Teodor (her buyer, the only one who calls her "Miss"). Cross-quartet: Sera (she worships her, which irritates Sera), Wren (both worship Sera, which irritates them both; and the secret). See `groups/salt-pans.md`.
- Alt policy: same person in a new role only (WB §8). Possible return: Corra, the Counter, if she ends up on the rooftops with the town in Act IV, counting instead of picking (working idea).

## Signature
- Signature item: a salt rake with the tines bent where she dug glass out of the pan, and a pocketful that clinks
- Victory line: "Pick it up, it's worth something!"
- Death line: "Tell Mum... it was salt money."
- Battle role: burst. Kit idea: collects shards from every enemy that falls; ultimate *Pocketful* throws them all at once, scaling with shards held, and costs her a little health ("glass cuts both ways").

## Sample hub lines
- (greeting) "Keep! Sera been up yet? Did she say anything? About me, I mean. Never mind."
- (idle) "Picked two handfuls this morning. That's Mum's boots. That's the rent. That's... well. That's mine."
- (tap) "Don't touch the pocket. It's not warm. It's just my pocket."

## Night of the fall
"Raking salt by lantern. It knocked me flat on my own pan. When I got up there was glass everywhere, so I picked it."

## Locked until
- **Canon fit:** Bram reached the crater first (WB §4, 000). Corra never went into the bowl. The fall threw glass across her pan, and she touched that before Bram got to the crater. That's how "first to touch the glass" (roster) and "Bram reached her first" both hold.
- The bond secret should come after the Opening's facts are settled on screen, and Wren should not learn it in a bond chapter (only the keeper does). If it ever reaches Wren, that's a group or main-story beat with a cause.
