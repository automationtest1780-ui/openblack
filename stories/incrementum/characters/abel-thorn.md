# Abel Thorn, the Reed-Cutter

Status: proposed (Claude, on user's delegation, 2026-10-06)
Pullable: yes (Salt Pans quartet)
Source: roster.md (Quartet 3); world-bible.md §2, §8. Where this sheet and the world bible differ, the world bible wins.

- Role: old reed-cutter who has worked the marsh channels for fifty years; battle role is control
- Group: the Salt Pans (the marsh). One side of the fault line: **bury the glass**
- Rarity and release: standard rarity (suggested). Launch standard pool, featured alongside Corra (1.0, phase 2). In the first-summon pool.
- Banner fantasy: the old man who's right to be afraid. Grim, steady, and kind to anything that can't speak.
- Teaser (proposed): *"Marsh gives. Marsh keeps. Put it back."*
- Want: the glass buried and the crater filled in before it takes something from the marsh
- Need: to let the young decide their own risks, and to say what he's afraid of in plain words
- Wound: He saw the reeds lean before the light fell, told nobody in time, and hasn't slept a night through since.
- Contradiction: preaches that the marsh can't be fought, and spends his nights fighting it by hand
- Voice — would say: "Marsh gives, marsh keeps." / "Put it back." / "Child." (for anyone under forty) / "I've seen it before. Not this. But before." / to the pony: "There's my girl."
- Voice — would never say: "it's probably nothing" / "money" without a pause before it / a joke (he only makes them by accident, and only to animals) / "the sky" casually (he says "up there" and doesn't look) / anything hurried
- Verbal tic: an old marsh saying first, then the plain meaning ("Marsh gives, marsh keeps. Meaning, put it back.")
- Silence: what he saw before the fall. He'll say "The reeds knew," and nothing after.
- Hook behavior: runs a thumb along the edge of his reed-hook before he speaks, testing it
- Bond secret: Every night he walks out to the crater and ties the leaning reeds upright to stakes, by hand, one by one. By morning they're leaning again. He's done it every night since the fall. *(Why: his fear comes out sideways, as labor. It invents nothing about the sky beyond the canon leaning reeds.)*
- Pairs: Corra (the fault line: sell vs bury; he taught her to cut reeds), Mags (thirty years running the pans together), Teodor (distrusts him; his pony likes him). Cross-quartet: Ivo (both distrust Teodor, for different reasons). See `groups/salt-pans.md`.
- Alt policy: same person in a new role only (WB §8).

## Signature
- Signature item: his grandfather's reed-hook, honed so thin it bends. His old pony, Patch, pulls the reed cart (local color).
- Victory line: "Marsh keeps what it's given. Go home."
- Death line: "Reeds... are leaning. Mind them."
- Battle role: control. Kit idea: slows any enemy near him; ultimate *Bury* sinks the enemy front rank into the channel, rooting them for a turn.
