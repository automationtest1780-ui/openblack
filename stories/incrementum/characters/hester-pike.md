# Hester Pike, the Smokewife

Status: proposed (Claude, on user's delegation, 2026-10-06)
Pullable: yes (Net Lofts quartet)
Source: roster.md (Quartet 2); world-bible.md §2, §8. Where this sheet and the world bible differ, the world bible wins.

- Role: runs the half-empty smokehouse on the harbor; battle role is healer / support
- Group: the Net Lofts (the fleet). The heart: holds the middle between leave and stay
- Rarity and release: standard rarity (suggested). Launch standard pool, featured alongside Nell (1.0, phase 1). In the first-summon pool.
- Banner fantasy: the hearth that knows everything. Fed, scolded, forgiven.
- Teaser (proposed): *"Tell me or don't. I'll know by Thursday."*
- Want: to keep the fleet fed and the town in one piece until the fish come back
- Need: to ask for something for herself, once
- Wound: She's been banking the same fires since her mother died at the hearth, and she can't remember the last time anyone fed her.
- Contradiction: keeps everyone's secrets and lets every debt slide, but never forgives anyone who gives up on Saltreach, including herself
- Voice — would say: "On the slate." / "You daft sprat." / "Out with it." / "I don't write secrets down. Only debts." / "Who's asking?"
- Voice — would never say: "love" (that's Bram's word) / "please" when she means it / "I'll collect" / "I don't know what you mean" (she always does) / a speech about the sky
- Verbal tic: fish names for people she's fond of ("sprat", "haddock", "you great cod"); everything owed goes "on the slate"
- Silence: about herself. Ask how she is and she answers about whoever ate last.
- Hook behavior: wipes her hands on her apron twice before she speaks, whether they need it or not
- Bond secret: She lost her sense of smell years ago, from the smoke. She judges every fish by its color and by the faces people make when they eat it. Nobody in Saltreach knows. *(Why: earthy, ironic, a little sad, and it explains why she watches faces so closely, which is why everyone confesses to her.)*
- Pairs: Nell (old friends; Nell's crew is the biggest tab), Tam (she half raised him), Jory (the Flint family debt is on her slate and she's never mentioned it). Cross-quartet: Mags (two women keeping two halves of the town fed). See `groups/net-lofts.md`.
- Alt policy: same person in a new role only (WB §8).

## Signature
- Signature item: her mother's iron fire-hook. The smokehouse fires haven't gone out in thirty-one years.
- Victory line: "That's paid, then. Wash your hands."
- Death line: "Don't... let the fires go out."
- Battle role: healer / support. Kit idea: heals over time from banked smoke that also blinds enemies; passive *On the Slate* stores overhealing as credit an ally can draw on later.
