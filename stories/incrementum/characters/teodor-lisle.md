# Teodor Lisle, the Buyer

Status: proposed (Claude, on user's delegation, 2026-10-06)
Pullable: yes (Salt Pans quartet)
Source: roster.md (Quartet 3, Links, Reveal guardrails); world-bible.md §2 (Bellwater). Where this sheet and the world bible differ, the world bible wins.

- Role: soft-spoken glass-buyer from Bellwater, lodging in Saltreach; battle role is support / debuff
- Group: the Salt Pans (the marsh). The question: do they let him, and the glass, leave Saltreach?
- Rarity and release: top rarity (suggested). Featured banner last of the four headliners (1.1, phase 2), after Abel, Mags and Corra have mentioned him (structure notes, item 6). He's in Saltreach from Act I, so he should be met in story before his banner. In the first-summon pool. Never needed in the main story to be owned (Hard rule 1).
- Banner fantasy: the gentle stranger who knows more than he says
- Teaser (proposed): *"Skies move, Keeper. Most towns simply don't look."*
- Want: to buy every piece of crater glass the marsh will sell him, at a fair price
- Need: to stop arriving after things happen (unspoken; Part 2 material)
- Wound: He arrived nine days after the fall, and whatever made him late, or made him come at all, he hasn't forgiven himself for. *(Writer-facing; never explained in Part 1.)*
- Contradiction: pays more than anyone for the glass, and seems to want it less than anyone
- Voice — would say: "Ah." / "Of course." / "If you'll allow me." / "Miss Vey", "Mr Thorn", "Mrs Teller", "Magistrate" (titles always) / "Where I'm from..." and then talks about the weather
- Voice — would never say: "Bloom" / "I'm surprised" / a contraction when he's being careful / anything short and blunt like Nell or Sera / what Bellwater knows
- Verbal tic: long, courteous, southern sentences in the conditional ("I should think", "one might say"); he praises an object before he names a price
- Silence: home. Ask about Bellwater and he tells you about its weather.
- Hook behavior: touches the small brass bell on his watch-chain without ringing it (its clapper is tied silent with black thread)
- Bond secret: He hasn't shipped a single piece of glass south. Everything he's bought is in two cases under his bed, each piece labeled with the date and its weight. He weighs them all again every night and writes it down. He won't say what he's checking for. *(Why: a hidden measurement, an echo of Ivo's ledger and of Bellwater's older lie (WB §2), without stating anything. Whether the weights change is not decided; never answer it in Part 1.)*
- Pairs: Corra (buyer and seller), Abel (distrust; the pony likes Teodor), Mags (business partners). Cross-quartet: Ivo (who notes he arrived nine days late, and who distrusts him for different reasons than Abel). See `groups/salt-pans.md`.
- Alt policy: same person in a new role only (WB §8). His natural return is in Bellwater's arc (Part 2), in a changed role (working idea).

## Signature
- Signature item: a small brass hand-bell on his watch-chain, clapper tied with black thread
- Victory line: "Ah. Well paid, I think."
- Death line: "I should have... come sooner."
- Battle role: support / debuff. Kit idea: passive *Pays Well* gives energy to the last ally who acted, at a cost to his own; ultimate *Quiet Bell* delays every enemy's next action.

## Sample hub lines
- (greeting) "Keeper, good evening. A fine room. The glass alone must be eighty years old. I'd not sell it."
- (idle, Act II+) "In Bellwater we have a saying too: don't look up after supper. How curious that you have it."
- (tap) "The bell? A keepsake. It's quite quiet. I prefer it that way."
