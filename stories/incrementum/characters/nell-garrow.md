# Nell Garrow, the Skipper

Status: proposed (Claude, on user's delegation, 2026-10-06)
Pullable: yes (Net Lofts quartet)
Source: roster.md (Quartet 2); world-bible.md §2, §8. Where this sheet and the world bible differ, the world bible wins.

- Role: captain of *Patience*, the last big herring boat in Saltreach; battle role is vanguard
- Group: the Net Lofts (the fleet). One side of the fault line: **leave**
- Rarity and release: top rarity (suggested). Headliner of the Net Lofts; featured banner at launch (1.0, phase 1). In the first-summon pool.
- Banner fantasy: command. The captain who makes the call and gets everyone out.
- Teaser (proposed, for marketing): *"Saltreach is finished. I'm just the first one saying it out loud."*
- Want: to take the fleet south before winter, where the herring went
- Need: to admit she's running from the night she lost her bearing, not toward the fish
- Wound: The night the light fell, she looked up for the Belt over her bow and, for the first time in thirty years at sea, didn't know where she was.
- Contradiction: preaches cutting losses and leaving sentiment ashore, but hasn't been able to take down her grandfather's steering board with its wrong pegs
- Voice — would say: "Done." / "That's settled." / "Haul." / "She'll do it" (about *Patience*) / people by surname: "Rook", "Hollis", "Flint"
- Voice — would never say: "Let's talk it over" / "I'm not sure" / "home" about Saltreach (she says "here") / "lost" about herself / a number of degrees (that's Sera's language; Nell steers by feel and says "a hand off", "a point to port")
- Verbal tic: ends arguments by declaring them over ("Done.") whether they are or not. Short orders, no softeners.
- Silence: never speaks about the night of the fall. If it comes up, she fills her pipe and doesn't light it.
- Hook behavior: slaps a flat hand on the nearest rail, table or barrel before she speaks, like calling a crew to attention
- Bond secret: She wrote south in the spring and was offered a winter berth for *Patience* in a southern port. She has never answered the letter. It lives folded in her coat, and she rereads it most nights on the boat. *(Why: the loudest leaver hasn't actually left. Mundane, a little sad, and it keeps the fault line open without settling it.)*
- Pairs: Tam (the fault line: leave vs stay), Hester (old friends, the biggest tab on the slate), Jory (she's about to do what his father did). Cross-quartet: Bram (grew up on the same slip). See `groups/net-lofts.md`.
- Alt policy: same person in a new role only (WB §8). Possible return: Nell, the Pilot, after Act IV, when she steers by Saltreach's own lights instead of the stars (working idea).

## Signature
- Signature item: the steering board from *Patience*'s wheelhouse, a peg-board of the Belt's bearing for each month, carved by her grandfather. Every peg is now wrong. She hasn't taken it down.
- Victory line: "Lines in. Nobody overboard. Done."
- Death line: "Take her... home. Whichever way that is."
- Battle role: vanguard. Kit idea: holds the front and draws fire; ultimate *Come About* swings the enemy line around, exposing their back rank.

## Night of the fall
"Coming home late. Looked for the Belt over the bow and couldn't find myself. Then the sky dropped something and I steered by that."
