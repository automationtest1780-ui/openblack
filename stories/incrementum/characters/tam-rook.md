# Tam Rook, the Netmender

Status: proposed (Claude, on user's delegation, 2026-10-06)
Pullable: yes (Net Lofts quartet)
Source: roster.md (Quartet 2); world-bible.md §2, §8. Where this sheet and the world bible differ, the world bible wins.

- Role: net-mender in the harbor lofts, about nineteen; battle role is shield
- Group: the Net Lofts (the fleet). One side of the fault line: **stay**
- Rarity and release: standard rarity (suggested). Launch standard pool, featured alongside Nell (1.0, phase 1). In the first-summon pool.
- Banner fantasy: gentleness that doesn't break. Nothing is past mending while he's here.
- Teaser (proposed): *"It's only torn. Torn's not gone."*
- Want: for Saltreach to stay, and for nobody to have to leave it
- Need: to accept that some things can't be mended, and to love them anyway
- Wound: He watched the bad year empty two houses on his street and couldn't do a thing about it with a needle.
- Contradiction: believes anything can be mended, but has never once gone past the breakwater to see what's out there
- Voice — would say: "That'll mend." / "Can I see it?" / "Sit still." / "Your cuff's going" (he notices what's torn on people before their faces) / "Well, that's a new one."
- Voice — would never say: "It's ruined" / "Give up" / a cruel joke on purpose (his jokes are accidents) / anything about the south he hasn't been told by someone else / a long speech
- Verbal tic: earnest, literal answers that come out funny ("Is the sky broken?" "Couldn't say. I've never had it in my lap.")
- Silence: when something truly can't be mended, he keeps his hands working and says nothing at all
- Hook behavior: takes the netting needle out from between his teeth before he speaks, so his first word is often half a mumble
- Bond secret: He ties a tiny knot of red thread into every net that leaves the loft, low in a corner where no skipper looks. If a net ever washes back without its boat, he'll know whose it was. He has never told anyone why. *(Why: his "everything mends" has a fear sewn into it. Fear comes out sideways, as a habit.)*
- Pairs: Nell (the fault line: leave vs stay), Hester (she half raised him; he mends her aprons), Jory (boyhood friend; forgives him too easily). Cross-quartet: Wren (he offers to take in Bram's coat; she won't let him). See `groups/net-lofts.md`.
- Alt policy: same person in a new role only (WB §8). Possible return: Tam, past the breakwater, if an event takes him out to sea for the first time (working idea).

## Signature
- Signature item: a whalebone netting needle worn to the shape of his hand, wound with red thread
- Victory line: "There. Good as new. Better, maybe."
- Death line: "Can't... reach the last knot."
- Battle role: shield. Kit idea: casts a net over allies that soaks damage and re-knits itself each turn it isn't broken; passive repairs other heroes' broken shields (pairs well with Bram).
