# Jory Flint, the Returner

Status: proposed (Claude, on user's delegation, 2026-10-06)
Pullable: yes (Net Lofts quartet)
Source: roster.md (Quartet 2); project.md (Protagonist); ledger ("previous breakwater keeper's family left in the bad year"); characters/ivo-thane.md (bond secret). Where this sheet and the world bible differ, the world bible wins.

- Role: son of the last breakwater keeper, home alone after his family left in the bad year; battle role is burst / striker
- Group: the Net Lofts (the fleet). The question: does Saltreach take him back?
- Rarity and release: top rarity (suggested). Featured banner after Nell and Corra (1.1, phase 1), so existing heroes can mention him first (structure notes, item 6). In the first-summon pool. Never needed in the main story to be owned (Hard rule 1).
- Banner fantasy: the prodigal. The stranger who came back, and won't say why.
- Teaser (proposed): *"Don't ask why I came back. I'm still deciding."*
- Want: to find whoever has been sending his mother money every month, unsigned, and pay back every coin
- Need: to let the town take him back without settling his father's account first
- Wound: His family left Saltreach in the night, owing half the harbor, and he was the one sent to tell the smokehouse they wouldn't be paying.
- Contradiction: insists he owes this town nothing, and came back alone to pay what his family owes it
- Voice — would say: "Not my business." / "Does it matter?" / "Keeper." (with an edge: it was his father's job) / "this place" / "Suits you."
- Voice — would never say: "home" about Saltreach / "my father" (he says "the old keeper") / "thank you" without a pause in front of it / why he came back / "I missed it"
- Verbal tic: says "Not my business" and then makes it his business
- Silence: the reason he came back. Ask, and he answers a different question.
- Hook behavior: glances at the breakwater light (his father's light, now the keeper's) before he speaks, as if checking it's lit
- Bond secret: He carries every unsigned envelope his mother received in the bad year's wake, tied in string, and not one coin spent. He came back to find the sender and give it all back: "We don't take charity from people who won't sign." *(Why: it's the question he won't answer. The money is Ivo's, which is Ivo's bond secret, so a keeper who has done both bonds knows more than either man. Jory must not learn the sender in Part 1 without an on-screen cause; see Locked until.)*
- Pairs: Nell (she's about to do what his father did), Tam (boyhood friend who forgives too easily), Hester (the Flint debt she never mentions). Cross-quartet: Ivo (the money; Jory doesn't know), and the Keeper (the job he grew up inside). See `groups/net-lofts.md`.
- Alt policy: same person in a new role only (WB §8). Possible return: Jory, the Lamp-Hand, if the story ever has him tend a light again (working idea).

## Signature
- Signature item: the return half of a packet-boat ticket, folded soft. He bought it the day he arrived and hasn't used it.
- Victory line: "Still here. Don't make a thing of it."
- Death line: "Should've... stayed on the boat."
- Battle role: burst / striker. Kit idea: passive *Alone* hits harder with no ally beside him; ultimate *One Way* dives to the enemy back rank, strikes, and comes back.
