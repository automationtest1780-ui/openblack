# Act III handoff sheet (for writers)

Fixed by Claude on 2026-10-07 before drafting Act III in parallel. Read `act3-starting-state.md` first; it describes where everyone is at the end of Act II (evening of day 23). Canon files win over this sheet; this sheet wins over the roadmap where they differ.

## Clock
Act II ran days 16–23 (8 days), so **Act III runs days 24–28** (about 4 days plus the quartet scene). The shifts keep speeding up, and then, on the finale night, the due shift doesn't come.

| Event | When | Belt (dusk bearing) | Wren after |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6th shift | **about midnight, night of day 24** (3-1) | 20 → 16 | **Brightening:** an older teen, a faint glow in the dark; the sleeves end mid-forearm |
| Spring tide floods the customs house | **afternoon of day 25** (3-2) | 16 | — |
| 7th shift | **dusk, day 26**, while Abel's crowd is at the crater (3-3) | 16 → 12 | brighter; she can see in the dark |
| The due shift that never comes | **night of day 27** (3-5): Sera and Ivo count aloud together, and the Belt holds at 12 until dawn | 12 holds | — |

**Tide:** spring tides with the drift on top. Nobody prints tables. In 3-1, Sera finally reads the keeper's dusk and three o'clock readings in the breakwater log (that's its scene). From then on she reads the log every morning.

## The ledger (what it is)
A thick black book in Ivo's hand, the "hidden ledger" of the world bible. For years he has taken a navigator's sight every clear night, a habit from his sailing days, and logged the Belt, the Nail and the harbor tide. It holds:
- the bad year's early tides, including the night the herring boat went down on the bar, when the tide came early. **Old Flint's light was lit; the tide was wrong.** That clears Jory's father (OQ 12).
- the first move of the Belt, two weeks before Sera saw it.
- every night since.

Its last page is always blank (WB). The book isn't handed over in Act III; that's Act V. After the confession it stays council property, open in the customs house. In 3-3, Ivo has a fair copy of the sky figures made for Sera and sends it up the stair with the keeper.

## Chapters and handoffs

**3-1 The Thicker Book** (day 24, into the small hours of 25)
- Starts: morning of day 24, the lamp room. Sera reads the breakwater log's readings since day 18 (she sees how far off the tide has been, and who wrote it). Bram and Sera are still raw, with supper but no deal.
- Owns: **the first Haze-Walkers** at dusk (Plumbs that *walk* through the haze in the lanes, slow, straight, silent; Act III variant). After hours in Hester's back room, Ivo puts the black ledger in front of Bram and says he'll read it to the council at tomorrow's session. Bram asks for one day to tell Sera himself. Nell comes in mid-scene wanting Bram to crew *Patience* south, and leaves without knowing what she walked past. Hester hears nothing she'll repeat. **The 6th shift at about midnight:** Wren brightens, and for the first time she says she *remembers* something about up there, one small concrete sensory memory, no explanation. Bram, in the lamp room, can't get the words out to Sera. He tries twice. The keeper knows.
- Ends: small hours of day 25. Bram hasn't told Sera. **Ivo has not said "I'm sorry"** (that's 3-3).

**3-2 Spring Tide in Session** (day 25)
- Owns: the council session in the customs house in the afternoon. The spring tide comes in hours early and floods the room mid-session, and the town crowds in to shout (flood fight; Tide-Plumbs in the water). Ivo stands in the water and opens the ledger in open session. He reads back the years to the bad year: the night the boat went down, the tide came early and Flint's light was lit. He reads the first move, two weeks before Sera. The room goes silent at the worst possible time (a held, silent shot). Sera is there. She **says nothing** in this chapter after the reading; her direct words to Ivo come in 3-3. She sees that Bram already knew. Jory hears his father cleared and goes out without a word. Nell takes it as a reason to leave and sets a date: "**the thirtieth, first light**". Ivo takes down "Don't look up after supper" and "Weather" from the door himself.
- Ends: night of day 25. The town knows, and the ledger lies open on the council table.

**3-3 The Council Breaks** (night of day 25 to dusk of day 26)
- Owns: three seats walk out (the harbormaster, the chandler and one more; Gage stays), and the harbor splits into camps overnight (leave with the fleet / fill the crater / stay and count). Late on the night of day 25, Sera finds Ivo alone in the flooded customs house. **Her first direct words to him since the haze.** The fault line breaks open. He says **"I'm sorry"** for the first time, and it doesn't help. Bram refuses to take a side, and says so to both. Ivo has the clerk's fair copy of the sky figures sent up the stair with the keeper. At dawn on day 26, Abel leads a crowd out to fill the crater. Teodor mildly offers to buy every piece of glass and take it south; Ivo notes he's surprised by none of it. **The 7th shift at dusk, at the crater**, with Haze-Walkers among the crowd. The crater isn't filled.
- Ends: night of day 26. Sera has the fair copy, Bram still hasn't picked a side, and Corra was at the crater.

**3-4 The Word for It** (night of day 26 into day 27)
- Owns: Wren walks out to the crater at night and Corra follows. Wren says the sky is opening "like the thrift on the headland" and calls it **the bloom**. She's the first to say it, with Corra as the only witness, who believes her. Mags counts heads when Haze-Walkers come down on the pans. Back in the lamp room, Sera lays the fair copy beside her wall, the parts she can bear to read, and finds that **between shifts the Belt creeps a little, except on the nights both she and Ivo counted, when the creep is almost nothing**. That's an observation only (OQ 4); nobody explains it. Sera writes "bloom" on the lamp-room glass. **The count moves to paper** (Sera's tide book and the fair copy), and Ivo's "paper" pays off without him there to enjoy it. Sera works out from the shrinking gaps that the next shift is due **tomorrow night**, the worst yet.
- Ends: day 27, before dusk.

**3-5 Two Records** (night of day 27, Act III finale, first anniversary)
- Owns: the worst night due. Sera goes to Ivo, or sends for him, to count together. Ivo, who took down the door notices, tells the town to **look up** (the reverse of his own saying). They carry the fair copy and the ledger up the stair, and Sera and Ivo count aloud together in the lamp room, with Bram holding the light and Wren between them. The town watches from the harbor wall, and every launch hero gets a line. **The due shift doesn't come. The Belt holds at 12 until dawn.** The town sees it. Best production of Part 1 so far: a CG of the still sky, credits over the harbor wall. A first thaw between Sera and Bram with an on-screen cause (he holds the light where she needs it, and she says the deal's first half). The post-Act III Sera/Ivo bark switches on at the end ("Call it." / "...Thank you. Left.") or is set up.
- Ends: dawn of day 28.

**Quartet scene 3, "Two Records"** (evening of day 28)
- The wall and the ledger side by side in the lamp room. Nobody speaks for a long time. Bram finally does (WB §6). No plot that exists nowhere else.

## Rules that hold all act
- **Reveal gate Act III:**
  - **Allowed:** Ivo knew; the ledger, years deep and tying the bad year to the sky; a new star forming (Wren's "opening", the thrift); the word "bloom" from 3-4 on (Wren first; others only after 3-4); measurement *seems* to slow it (observation only).
  - **Still locked:** Wren is the Bloom's child, and the sky is calling her (Act IV); growth can't be undone; the ending; the old lamp lit.
  - **Never:** an explanation of why counting holds the sky.
- Plumbs, Tide-Plumbs, Haze-Walkers and Glass Hounds never speak. The Bloom never speaks in words. Wren hearing it is her own sense of it.
- Wren is **Brightening**. Her voice slows and sometimes comes "from far up"; she still ends scary lines on a question or a joke. She remembers small sensory things (cold, wide, quiet, a crowd of lights). She doesn't know *what* she is.
- Ivo's first "I'm sorry" is in 3-3 and nowhere before it. He still doesn't use contractions under pressure.
- Sera speaks directly to Ivo for the first time in 3-3. Before that, she uses others or stays silent.
- Bond secrets stay secret: Bram's oil cupboard, Sera's tally, Wren's marks under the stair, Ivo's money, Jory's envelopes, Teodor's cases. (Jory learning his father was cleared is a main-story reveal, not his bond secret.)
- The old lamp stays dark.
