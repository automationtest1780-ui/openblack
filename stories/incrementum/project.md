# Incrementum

Bible: gacha
World bible: `world-bible.md` (v0.1 working draft, Spencer's). Kept verbatim; never edit it. If it changes, replace the file and re-check the derived files below.

## Precedence
1. **World bible, Canon material**: fixed. Never contradict, never rephrase in a way that changes meaning.
2. **World bible, working material**: use it by default. It may be revised, but only by the user.
3. **Gacha bible**: governs how the story is written (structure, dialogue, pacing, review).
4. **This folder's derived files** (character sheets, ledger): convenience views of 1–2. When they disagree with the world bible, the world bible wins; fix the derived file.

**Invention policy (world bible, "How to Use"):** if neither bible covers something that matters, keep it mysterious on screen and add it to `open-questions.md`; don't make up lore. Purely local color (a net-loft gossip's name, what's in tonight's soup) is fine, as long as it doesn't create a fact anyone must remember.

## Premise sentence (gacha §9)
Proposed: *Plain-spoken working people of a cold harbor town face a sky that has started to move. Every hero is a person of a place first and a fighter second, and the sky is never explained by anyone who speaks like a prophet.*

## Spine sentence (gacha §3)
Proposed: *When a growing star pulls on the ground of Saltreach, four people who refuse to look away gather in the dead lighthouse to count, shield and fight the Plumbs, but each shift grows Wren further from the girl they found, until the sky gets a new fixed point.*

## Protagonist
- Role: **the player.** The world bible addresses them at home base ("You're back. Good. Hold the chalk.") but never says who they are in-world. See open question 9.
- Voiced: no. They speak little (gacha §6).

## Campaign structure
- Opening (pre-gacha) + Acts I–V = the **spine** (gacha layer "Spine chapter"). Act and Wren-stage gating follow world bible §3 "What Players Learn and When" and §4 Wren arc.
- Quartet scenes (world bible §6): a room, not spine. Must not hold plot found nowhere else.
- Pair-bond conversations: gacha "Bond / relationship" layer, under 300 words (world bible §8; gacha §12's 120–250 fits inside that).
- Future regions (Ferrow, Bellwater, Mount Quell): future quartets; open hooks only.

## Tone (world bible §8, overrides gacha §9 default where they differ)
Plain speech; people talk like workers. Understatement is the house style. Fear comes out sideways: jokes (Bram), numbers (Sera, Ivo), silence. No villain monologues. The Bloom has no words, and Plumbs never speak. Every scene is about a person first and the sky second.
