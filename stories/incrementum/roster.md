# Launch roster (Part 1: Saltreach)

Decided by Claude on 2026-10-06, on the user's delegation ("fill our gaps"). Spencer may override any of it. Built with the world bible's own method, §8 "Building New Quartets": a place, four roles, one fault line, one heart, one question.

Each quartet experiences the moving sky its own way. Saltreach launches with **12 heroes in 3 quartets**: the Lamp Room four, free through the story, plus two pullable quartets. The first summon is a guaranteed pick from the eight pullable heroes, framed as "someone else in Saltreach who saw the light fall." Rarity is set by design; the suggestion is two top-rarity heroes per pullable quartet.

Names follow WB §8: Saltreach names are clipped and coastal. The one outsider (Teodor Lisle, from Bellwater) has a softer, longer southern sound, which sets Bellwater's regional flavor.

## Quartet 1: The Lamp Room (free, starter)
Sera Vant, Bram Hollis, Ivo Thane, Wren. Fault line: truth vs protection (Sera / Ivo). Heart: Bram. Question: Wren. See world bible §4–6.

## Quartet 2: The Net Lofts (the fleet)
**Place:** the harbor, the net lofts, the smokehouse, the boats. **How they feel the sky:** they steer by the Belt. The bad year already broke them once, and now the stars they navigate by are wrong.
**Fault line:** *leave vs stay.* Does the fleet go south, where the herring went, or does Saltreach hold?
**Heart:** Hester. **Question:** Jory.

| Hero | One line | Night of the fall |
|---|---|---|
| **Nell Garrow, the Skipper** | Captain of *Patience*, the last big herring boat. She's sure Saltreach is finished and wants to take the fleet south before winter. Blunt, generous, impatient with sentiment. | At sea, coming home late. She saw it fall over her bow and lost her bearing for the first time in her life. |
| **Tam Rook, the Netmender** | A young net-mender who has never been past the breakwater and never wants to. He believes anything torn can be mended, including a town. Gentle, stubborn, funny without meaning to be. | On the net-loft roof, mending by moonlight. He watched it fall and kept mending. |
| **Hester Pike, the Smokewife** | Runs the half-empty smokehouse. She feeds the fleet on credit and keeps everyone's secrets, plus a ledger of debts she'll never collect. Earthy, sharp-tongued, the one people confess to. | Banking the smokehouse fires. The flash came through the smoke vents. |
| **Jory Flint, the Returner** | Son of the last breakwater keeper, whose family left in the bad year. He has come back alone, and won't say why. The town can't decide whether to take him back. Some say his father left debts and a lie behind. | On the deck of the packet boat, coming home. He saw it fall over the town he'd sworn never to see again. |

Links: Jory's family is one of the two that Ivo secretly sends money to; Jory doesn't know who the sender is. The keeper (the player) now does Jory's father's old job. Nell and Bram grew up on the same slip.

## Quartet 3: The Salt Pans (the marsh)
**Place:** the salt pans and reed beds north of town, closest to the crater. **How they feel the sky:** the fall landed on their doorstep. The reeds lean, the Plumbs walk the channels, and the crater glass is worth money.
**Fault line:** *sell the glass vs bury it.* Crater glass is the first real money the marsh has seen in years. It's also clearly wrong.
**Heart:** Mags. **Question:** Teodor.

| Hero | One line | Night of the fall |
|---|---|---|
| **Corra Vey, the Glass-Picker** | A salt-pan girl, about seventeen. She picks crater glass at dawn and sells it, the first money her family has had in years. Quick, bright, defiant, guilty underneath. | Raking salt by lantern near the crater. It knocked her flat. She was the first to touch the glass, before Bram got there. |
| **Abel Thorn, the Reed-Cutter** | An old reed-cutter who reads the marsh like scripture. He wants the glass buried and the crater filled in, and says out loud what others whisper. Grim, devout, unexpectedly tender with animals. | Cutting reeds at dusk. He saw the reeds lean before the light fell, and he hasn't slept well since. |
| **Mags Teller, the Pan-Boss** | Runs the salt pans: wages, shares, quarrels. She keeps the marsh fed and fair. Practical, funny, tired, holds the middle. | Counting the day's salt in the pan-house. She ran out with a lamp to count heads. |
| **Teodor Lisle, the Buyer** | A soft-spoken glass-buyer from Bellwater who pays well and asks few questions. The marsh must decide whether to let him, and the glass, leave Saltreach. He seems unsurprised by a moving sky. | Not in Saltreach. He arrived nine days later, which is the first thing Ivo notes about him. |

Links: Teodor is the door to Bellwater, "a quartet who have been hiding a moving sky for a generation" (WB §2). What he knows stays locked until Bellwater's arc, and he never says it outright in Part 1. Abel and Ivo both distrust him, for different reasons. Corra worships Sera the way Wren does, which irritates them both.
