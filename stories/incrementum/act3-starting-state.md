# Act III starting state (end of Act II, evening of day 23)

Written by Claude on 2026-10-06 after the Act II continuity pass, to brief the Act III writers. It summarizes canon. The ledger, the sheets and patches 201-205 win over this page; the world bible wins over all of them.

## The sky and the sea
- **Belt:** dusk bearing **20** (old notch 41). Shifts so far: before night three (41 → 37), dusk day 13 (36 → 32), about 2 a.m. on the night of day 17 (32 → 28), dusk day 20 (28 → 24), dusk day 22 (24 → 20). The gaps are 16 days, 4, about 2¾, 2. The next one is due soon. Nobody on screen has said when.
- **Tide:** Sera's last figures are **2 h 10** (the bar, night of day 17) and the **2 h 40, still rising** on the leaf. By day 21 it came in at dusk against a table time of nine. The tables are "not usable", struck through, and nobody has printed new ones. The keeper's dusk and three o'clock readings since day 18 are in the breakwater log, and **Sera hasn't read them yet**. When she does, it should be a scene. Nell has read them.
- **The boats** mostly stay in. Nell will take the fleet south "when she's dry" (a decision, but no date).

## The wall and the count
- **The wall is full** (night of day 22). From the door, the last course reads: 19; 32, 32; 32, 31; 32, 52; 28, 2.10; 28; 28; 24; 24; Wren's long height line, "W. 21."; then 20 and the keeper's "1 finger".
- **Where counts go now:** on the back of the keeper's hand and Sera's own (day 23). The floor, the glass, the stair, paper (Ivo, four times) and Hester's pot lid have all been tried or refused, and Sera won't write on Wren's arm. "Tomorrow I'll find stone." This is open (OQ 38e).
- **Sera keeps the chalk herself** ("No. I'll hold it."). The keeper still sights and counts with her.
- **The leaf** is in the front of Sera's tide book. "2 h 40" was on the back of her hand.

## Wren
- **Kindling.** Teenage height, up to the keeper's eyebrows. The coat sleeves stop short of her wrists, with nothing left to roll. She was fifteen hands and a finger on day 21 and has grown past that line.
- The town says "she grows when the sky moves". Nobody says why. The harbor walks its children round her.
- She burned Tam's hands (day 20) and won't glow on purpose outside a fight. She itches before a shift ("It's going to step"). At the 5th shift she lit gold in the lamp room for 61 seconds, and every Plumb on the marsh stood still facing it.
- She is "Sparrow" to Bram. She sits in the middle of the stair when Sera and Bram quarrel. She offered her arm as the next wall and keeps her sleeve up "just in case". She is still trying to make Ivo smile (he wrote her down "Under 'almost.' On paper.").

## The Lamp Room four and the keeper
- **Sera:** right, and broken with Bram. She knows Bram hid the reading for "four days and nineteen hours" and that the keeper wrote it and kept quiet. She told Bram to "go and keep a light for a tower nobody uses". Her only words to him since are "I know" and "It's cold". She still won't speak to Ivo directly. She treated Wren as a reading (203), and on day 23 she couldn't do it again.
- **Bram:** caught, truthful once caught, and he'd "choose it again". He still brings supper every night (Sera ate it cold on day 23), and the tally still gets its stroke. He began their deal and couldn't finish it. He goes to the slip at three to look at the water, and has invited the keeper. Hester has him "on the slate" for it.
- **Ivo:** his ration and glass stop worked. The town is indoors after supper and the marsh hates him (Mags won't count him). He asked Abel for one night's silence about "measuring". He knows about the cut leaf (from Jory, and from Bram's quick agreement on the marsh) and told Bram "I would have cut it too" and "One gets used to it." He has never said "haze" in private or shown a sky ledger. He writes only in the green minute book and a plain notebook.
- **The keeper:** Sera's second witness, who held the chalk five dusks while keeping Bram's secret. They're on Hester's slate for it. Sera hasn't decided "what to do with that yet." Mags has them in the pan book "under road".

## The launch heroes
- **Nell:** decided on the south, no date. She thanked the keeper, not Sera.
- **Tam:** hands in honey and linen, weeks from mending, and itching now. He forgave Wren the same night.
- **Hester:** knows about the leaf and keeps it. She resents the council's use of "her" saying. She feeds the harbor at five.
- **Jory:** keeps the breakwater lamp while the keeper patrols and mends Tam's nets badly. He found the stub, brought the log up the tower, and had it signed at the customs house as a page short. He still won't say why he came back.
- **Mags:** told her cutters "no glass money" by name, and won't count Ivo.
- **Corra:** her family is on Hester's slate. She has the Belt's bearings (41 to 24) chalked on the pan-house door, "in case she runs out".
- **Abel:** first to say the Plumbs are "measuring something", and won't say what. He stakes the reeds every night, and they now lean toward the town.
- **Teodor:** his glass may stay with him but may not leave Saltreach. He's in no hurry. Bellwater "has the same saying".

## The town and the enemies
- "Weather. The council is watching it." and "Don't look up after supper." are both on the customs house door in black. Lamps are out by nine, and the lamp room is the only lit window after supper.
- **Plumbs** have stood in the harbor lanes (day 20), stood as **Tide-Plumbs** in the harbor and walked to the net-loft stair (day 21, seen by six people and one child), and stood in nineteen rows facing the tower (day 22). Rows turn when crossed. They stop at the edge of lamplight. They never speak.
