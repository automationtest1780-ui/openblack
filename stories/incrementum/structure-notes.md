# Incrementum: Game-Specific Structure Notes

These are the structural settings that used to live in the Incrementum world bible (v0.1, archived in `archive/`). They are project settings, not world canon. Each one is mapped to the gacha bible section that now governs it. Where a note differs from a gacha bible default, treat it as a recorded deviation (gacha bible section 0) unless the team drops it.
