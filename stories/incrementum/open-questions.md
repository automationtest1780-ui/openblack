# Open questions

Questions 1–8 are copied from world-bible.md §10 (for Spencer). The answers to 1–8 were decided by Claude on 2026-10-06, on the user's delegation ("fill our gaps"). Spencer may override any of them. New questions raised while writing are added below with the date and the piece that raised them. When one is answered, record the answer here and update the ledger or sheets.

1. The ending: "Wren grows into the new sky," an alternative, or player choice?
   - **Answer:** "Wren grows into the new sky" is the direction, and now locked. No player choice: a live-service game needs one canon so returns, events and Part 2 can build on it (gacha 10.3, 17). The alternatives stay as what the characters *fear* might happen in Acts IV–V.
2. How the ledger comes out: confession or exposure?
   - **Answer:** both, in that order. In mid Act III Ivo decides to confess and tells Bram first (WB §5). Before he can choose his moment, the harbor floods during a council session and the town panics. He confesses in open council anyway, at the worst possible time, because the town needs both records to hold the sky. His fear comes true and he chooses it regardless. That's the stronger arc.
3. Bram and Sera: does he say it on screen, and does she answer?
   - **Answer:** yes, on screen, in Act V, in plain words ("finally says what he never said", WB arc). Sera answers with a number: she opens the back of her tide book and reads out the supper tally, the bond secret players may already know. Nothing more is said. The quartet scene "The Blank Page" can show them sharing a bowl.
4. Is "measurement holds things in place" the right engine?
   - **Answer:** yes, kept as the engine, but never explained on screen. Characters only observe it: counting seems to slow the drift (Act III), and the town counting together holds it (Act IV). Nobody gives a lecture. This keeps the mystery and the no-prophets tone.
5. The Bloom: blind growth or a will?
   - **Answer:** blind growth, as drafted. Wren *hears* it as a voice from Act III because she's part of it. What she reports is her own reading, not the Bloom speaking, and the Bloom never speaks in words (WB tone rules). Characters may argue about whether it's aware; the writers know it isn't.
6. Wren's portrait art changing by act (art budget)?
   - **Answer:** yes. One base portrait per stage (5 total), swapped by the player's campaign progress, not real time. Splash art is needed only for her launch portrait and her post-ending return (Wren, the Fixed Star). Treat the other three as portrait swaps, not new splashes.
7. First summon: starter quartet guaranteed, then gacha? Or a first-pull guarantee?
   - **Answer:** the Lamp Room four are free through the story. The first summon is a guaranteed pick from the Saltreach launch roster (`roster.md`), framed in story as "someone else in Saltreach who saw the light fall." Every launch hero has a line about where they were that night.
8. Living title screen: campaign progress or real time?
   - **Answer:** the player's campaign progress, so every player's sky matches their story and nobody gets a spoiler. Live seasons may add an overlay (festival lanterns, snow) on top.
